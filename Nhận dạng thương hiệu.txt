NẾP NÓN – OFFICIAL BRAND GUIDELINE IMPLEMENTATION
You are implementing the official brand identity for NẾP NÓN.
Follow this brand guideline consistently across every page, component, image treatment, and customer interaction.
1. Brand identity
Brand name: NẾP NÓN
Primary tagline: Giữ nét đẹp Việt – Gửi trọn yêu thương.
Brand positioning:
A premium Vietnamese handmade conical hat brand rooted in traditional craftsmanship and family heritage in Thái Bình, Vietnam.
Brand personality:

* Elegant.
* Authentic.
* Warm.
* Traditional.
* Trustworthy.
* Contemporary.

Creative direction:
Heritage Luxury × Vietnamese Craft Heritage.
2. Official color tokens
Implement these exact CSS variables:
--color-forest: #263D25;
--color-grass: #405A35;
--color-ivory: #F8F4EA;
--color-wood: #8B6846;
--color-bamboo: #E8D9BD;
--color-text: #292820;
--color-muted: #777367;
--color-white: #FFFFFF;
Recommended usage:

* 50% ivory.
* 25% forest green.
* 15% bamboo beige.
* 7% secondary green.
* 3% warm brown.

Do not introduce unrelated colors without a clear design reason.
3. Typography
Use Google Fonts:
Headings: Lora.
Body: Be Vietnam Pro.
Decorative accent: Dancing Script, only where Vietnamese character support is verified.
Suggested typography scale:
Desktop:

* Hero title: 64–76px.
* Section heading: 36–44px.
* Product title: 18–22px.
* Body: 15–17px.
* Caption: 12–14px.

Mobile:

* Hero title: 36–44px.
* Section heading: 27–32px.
* Body: 14–16px.

Use proper font weights and line heights.
4. Logo system
Create a custom scalable SVG logo.
Logo concept:
A minimal, elegant line-art Vietnamese conical hat with carefully balanced radial lines.
Combine the icon with the NẾP NÓN wordmark.
Create:

* Primary horizontal logo.
* Stacked logo.
* Icon-only logo.
* Monochrome dark version.
* White/reverse version.

Requirements:

* Accurate Vietnamese typography.
* Transparent background.
* SVG vector format.
* Consistent stroke width.
* Appropriate clear space.
* Responsive scaling.

Never use an AI-generated raster logo as the final production logo.
5. Photography direction
Prioritize real photographs supplied by the business.
Create reusable image components for:

* Mother artisan portraits.
* Handmade product photography.
* Craftsmanship process.
* Vietnamese countryside.
* Lifestyle photographs.

Use:

* Natural lighting.
* Warm ivory tones.
* Authentic environments.
* Documentary-style photography.
* Elegant editorial compositions.

Never misrepresent generated images as real photographs of the mother or actual products.
6. Packaging visual system
Design mockups and reusable assets for:

* Kraft shopping bags.
* Conical hat packaging boxes.
* Product labels.
* Thank-you cards.
* QR code story cards.
* Gift packaging.

Use forest green, ivory, bamboo beige, and kraft textures.
7. Website UI system
Website appearance:
Premium, editorial, minimalist, Vietnamese heritage.
Apply:

* Generous whitespace.
* Elegant serif headlines.
* Warm neutral backgrounds.
* Forest green primary CTAs.
* Restrained borders and shadows.
* Consistent spacing.
* Beautiful image compositions.
* Subtle animations.

Avoid:

* Excessive rounded cards.
* Generic ecommerce templates.
* Excessive gradients.
* Unnecessary decorative effects.
* Overly commercial banners.

8. Reusable design tokens
Create a central design system.
Suggested files:
src/styles/tokens.css
src/styles/globals.css
src/components/brand/BrandLogo.tsx
src/components/brand/BrandStory.tsx
src/components/brand/BrandSectionHeading.tsx

Ensure all components use the shared design tokens.
9. Brand consistency validation
Before completing the project:

* Verify all pages use consistent colors.
* Verify logo proportions.
* Verify Vietnamese typography.
* Verify responsive layouts.
* Verify image aspect ratios.
* Verify button consistency.
* Verify accessibility and contrast.
* Verify the overall visual identity.

The final result must feel like one coherent premium Vietnamese artisan brand.
Do not create a generic ecommerce website.
Create a distinctive and emotionally engaging NẾP NÓN brand experience.
